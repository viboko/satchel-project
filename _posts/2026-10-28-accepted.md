This morning I had my interview with Jesse and Matthieu from the ODI about the
[Solid open call](/updates/2026-09-02-the-odi-solid-open-call/). We talked
through the [prototype and its code](/updates/2026-10-25-satchel-initial-investigations/)
and what the project will need from them. At the end, they told me they
think Satchel is a good fit, and that it has been accepted!

I'm really thrilled. An official acceptance email, with more of the details, will
follow.

## What happens next

According to the call, the work period starts on 2nd November and runs until
30th April 2027. It begins with two workshops: one on how Solid fits into
Satchel's architecture, and one on a roadmap for building it.

I now want to move beyond the demo to start implementing a structure closer to the proposed architecture, pulling the shared Satchel logic out to a Core package.

I'll keep posting updates here as things move...
