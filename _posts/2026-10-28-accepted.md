This morning I had my interview with the ODI about the
[Solid open call](/updates/2026-09-02-the-odi-solid-open-call/). We talked
through the [prototype and its code](/updates/2026-10-25-satchel-initial-investigations/)
and what the project will need from them. At the end, they told me they
think Satchel is a good fit, and that it has been accepted.

I'm thrilled. An official acceptance email, with more of the details, will
follow.

## What happens next

According to the call, the work period starts on 2 November and runs until
30 April 2027. It begins with two workshops: one on how Solid fits into
Satchel's architecture, and one on a roadmap for building it.

The thing I most want to get right early is how sharing works. A share
should only take effect once a child's guardian has approved it, it should
be possible to take it back at any time, and Solid's own access control
should enforce that, not just Satchel's code. That's where I'll be leaning
on the ODI's engineers first.

I'll keep posting updates here as things move.
