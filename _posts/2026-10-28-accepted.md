This morning I had my interview with Jesse and Matthieu from the ODI about
the [Solid open call](/blog/2026-09-02-the-odi-solid-open-call/). We talked
through the [prototype and its code](/blog/2026-10-25-initial-investigations/)
and what the project will need from them. At the end, they told me they
think Satchel is a good fit, and that it has been accepted!

I'm really thrilled. I also received an email to make it official:

> Dear Matthew,
>
> I’m pleased to let you know that you have successfully advanced past the
> technical interview stage of the Solid Social Good Open Call, and we would
> like to offer you a six-month support package to help further develop your
> idea.
{: .email-excerpt}

## What happens next

The work period starts on 2nd November and runs until 30th April 2027.

I now want to move beyond the demo to start implementing a structure closer
to the proposed architecture, pulling the shared Satchel logic out to a Core
package.

I'll keep posting updates here as things move...
