Some very exciting news today: the ODI emailed to say that
[my application](/application/) to the
[Solid open call](/updates/2026-09-02-the-odi-solid-open-call/) has passed
the first stage and gone through to the next round.

The next step is a 30-minute technical interview, which I've booked for next
week. It isn't a pitch, and applicants aren't competing against each other
at this stage. It's a conversation to understand the project and whether the
ODI can support it well: in the email's own words, "a conversation about
what you'll need from us".

That's a useful question to have to answer properly. Between now and the
interview I'll be working out exactly what I'd want from the ODI's
engineers, its hosted Solid servers and its wider network, and getting a
first working piece of Satchel ready to show.
