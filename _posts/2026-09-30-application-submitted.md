## The idea

The project is called **Satchel** (a working title). It lets a child's
[Scratch](/what-is-scratch/) projects be saved to their own
[Solid Pod](/what-is-solid/), rather than living only inside whichever
club, school or platform account they were made in.

{% include logo.html src="/assets/images/satchel-logo.svg" alt="The Satchel logo, a leather satchel bag" height="120" %}

On top of that sits a sharing dashboard, where the child and their guardian
decide *together* who else can see each project, and either of them can take
a share back at any time. That replaces Scratch's all-or-nothing "shared or
not" switch. A small, replaceable agent carries out their decisions, without
ever holding the children's work or anyone's password.
[Satchel: adding Solid storage to Scratch](/what-is-satchel/) explains it in
more detail.

Scratch is the concrete starting point, but the pattern underneath is meant
to be more general: a child's own, portable store of their learning work,
controlled by them and their family. [Beyond Scratch](/beyond-scratch/)
covers that, and [How this differs from previous attempts](/how-this-differs/)
explains why earlier learner-record projects struggled where Satchel might not.

## What I asked for

I applied at the **Start** tier: nothing is built yet, and the aim is a
proof of concept. I'll use synthetic data rather than real children's Pods.
The help I asked for is mostly technical: testing the design against real
Solid servers, and working out the access control for decisions that a child
and a guardian make together, which doesn't seem to have an established
answer in Solid yet.

## What happens next

Judging runs from 1st to 16th October, with interviews between 19th and 30th
October. Fingers crossed! 🤞
